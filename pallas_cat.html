Pallas's cat (Otocolobus manul)

Habitat and distribution

The Pallas's cat (Otocolobus manul) is distributed across a broad region of Central Asia, ranging from the Caucasus and the Iranian Plateau through Mongolia and southern Siberia to the Tibetan Plateau and parts of the Himalayas. It inhabits cold, dry environments such as rocky grasslands, montane steppes, shrublands, scree slopes, and high-altitude plateaus, typically in areas with limited snow cover. Well adapted to harsh continental climates, the species relies on rocky outcrops, crevices, and abandoned burrows for shelter and protection from predators and extreme weather.
