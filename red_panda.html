Cuty red panda ( Ailurus fulgens )

Habitat and distribution

Around the Himalayas; Nepal, Bhutan, India, China, and Myanmar are the lucky places to offer shelter to this cute and furocious species. You may find traces of their paws in around 140,000 sq km, with China being the largest potential habitat, followed by India.

Diet / behavior / social organization

Red pandas are omnivorous, but bamboos is what you will most usually see them have between their paws. When they do not chew on bamboo sticks, or the delicacy -bamboo flowers-, they express their snacki- ness on insects, bird eggs, small birds, rodents or lizards. Red pandas are solitary animals that are most active at dawn and dusk. They are excellent tree climbers and use their long, bushy tails for balance and warmth. They spend much of their time resting in trees.
