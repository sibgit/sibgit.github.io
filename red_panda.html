Red panda (Ailurus fulgens)

Habitat and distribution

Red pandas are found mainly in the temperate forests of the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China, including Nepal, India, Bhutan, Myanmar, and China.
They typically live in mountainous forests at elevations of about 1,500–4,800 metres, preferring dense bamboo undergrowth for food and shelter.

A. f. fulgens is mainly found in the eastern Himalayas, while A. f. styani occurs primarily in southwestern China and northern Myanmar.
