Diet

Red pandas primarily eat bamboo, especially tender leaves and young shoots. Because bamboo is low in nutrients, they consume about 20–30% of their body weight daily. They also eat fruits, berries, flowers, acorns, and occasionally insects, bird eggs, and small animals.

Behavior

Red pandas are solitary, arboreal (tree-dwelling), and primarily crepuscular (most active at dawn and dusk) or nocturnal.
