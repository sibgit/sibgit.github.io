Common octopus (Octopus vulgaris)

Habitat and distribution

Octopuses live in oceans worldwide, mainly on rocky or sandy seafloors, from shallow coastal waters to the deep sea.

Diet / behavior / social organization

Octopuses eat mainly crabs, shrimp, fish, shellfish, and other small marine animals. They are mostly solitary animals, living and hunting alone except during mating.
